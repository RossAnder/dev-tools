Verdict: A seventh surface is a new `Surface` variant (glimpse/src/surface.rs:16) plus its own state struct over the generic `Tracking<R>` (surface.rs:419) and a draw arm in `draw_surface` (glimpse/src/view/mod.rs:400). Sub-lists can take the `h`/`l` and Tab keys, which do nothing useful off Tasks. Jumps between records, a selectable details pane and a source preview pane have no precedent, so each needs new App state, a new Action and new Regions fields. The silent `_ =>` and `!= Surface::Tasks` arms are the trap: they compile and misroute the new surface.

## How it is built today

- **Surfaces.** `Surface` is a 6-variant enum. `Surface::ALL` sets the digit order: `from_digit`, `digit`, `label`, `key` (the `--surface` and `state.toml` name) and `ledger_kind`, `groups`, `sorts` are all exhaustive matches (surface.rs:26-179). `App.surface` holds the current one. `App.items: HashMap<Surface, ItemsState>` holds the four ledger surfaces, and `App.inbox: InboxState` holds the Inbox (glimpse/src/app.rs:380-385). `SwitchSurface` sets the surface and clears its arrival count (app.rs:902-911).
- **List rows.** `Tracking<R: Tracked>` (surface.rs:419-553) is the part shared between lists, keyed by row id: rows, revision, cursor, flashes, saving, scroll, pin, page, `step_cursor` and `page_through`. `ItemsState` (surface.rs:558) and `InboxState` (surface.rs:789) wrap it through `Deref` and add their own visible order (filter, group, sort, marks or sections). Drawing goes through `view/table.rs`: `Row<T>`, `draw_rows`, `header` and `cell` return the click rects. `view/items.rs::render` and `view/inbox.rs::render` both return an `items::Drawn`, which `draw_surface` turns into `Hits` and writes back as scroll and page (view/mod.rs:405-421).
- **Details pane.** `view/details.rs::render` (details.rs:33-69) matches on the surface. Tasks gets `content()` and every other surface gets the item cursor row's `item_content()`. Both build one unwrapped `Text`, which is wrapped and scrolled by `App.details_scroll`. Nothing in it can be selected. Related tasks (Needs, Coupling, Dependents, Shares files with, details.rs:176-202) are drawn as text only. `draw_panels` (view/mod.rs:452-491) lays out exactly two panels, details and activity, side by side or stacked. `split_body` opens the panel area on `details_open || activity_open` (view/mod.rs:269).
- **Keys.** `keys::map` does not depend on context (glimpse/src/keys.rs:22-97). `App::apply` gives an action its meaning per surface through match guards. On a non-Tasks surface `Move` becomes `ItemMove`, which returns `None` for Left and Right (app.rs:566-568, 912-916). `NextView` and `PrevView` (Tab) change the Tasks view whatever surface is showing (app.rs:573-584). `Back` is a fixed priority chain: marks, then legend, selector, full-screen details, details, activity, quit (app.rs:1114-1134). No key is mapped to `Backspace`. The free lowercase letters are `b` and `p`, and the free capitals include `A B E G I L M N O Q R U W X Y`. The mouse acts on the rects the last frame recorded in `Regions` (app.rs:108-125).

## What a seventh surface with sub-lists, jumps and a preview takes

1. A new variant in `Surface` and `ALL`, with an arm in every exhaustive match in surface.rs. `ledger_kind` returns `None` for it, so `state.rs::item_surfaces` (glimpse/src/state.rs:55-59) skips saving its arrangement unless that is changed.
2. A new state struct, for example one `Tracking<R>` per sub-list and an active sub-list index, held as a new `App` field. Each row type needs a `Tracked` impl (surface.rs:965-983 has the examples). It also needs an arm in `draw_surface` and a new `view/<name>.rs` that draws through `table.rs`.
3. Guarded arms in `apply` for `Move` Left and Right or `NextView` to switch sub-lists, for `ItemMove`, `Page`, `ScrollView`, `SelectItem` and `Details`, and in `details.rs::render`. Today every one of these falls into the item or Inbox branch (finding 1).
4. Jumps: a new `Action`, a back stack on `App` and a place for it in the `Back` chain, plus a way to choose a link (finding 3).
5. The preview: a third panel state in `draw_panels` and `split_body`, and its file read moved off the UI thread the way the transcript tail is (finding 4).
6. Literals and docs: `keys.rs:76` (`'1'..='6'`), the `"1-6"` hints at glimpse/src/view/footer.rs:325, 360 and 383, glimpse/src/view/legend.rs:56-57, glimpse/README.md:104 and 365, and the tests at keys.rs:605-619 (which assert that `'7'` maps to nothing), surface.rs:1274 and header.rs:534-554.
